3 Days Later

Short Film Writing Editing Sound

A five-person production "3 Days Later" is a thriller about a disappearance, a prime suspect, and a case built entirely on circumstantial evidence. Shot, scored and cut by our team of five over one semester.

I worked across the full pipeline: co‑writing the script, appearing on screen as the lead suspect, cutting the intro and several post‑production sequences, building AI‑generated video inserts, and shaping the sound design. Alongside the film itself, I designed the character and film posters and a promotional website.

The poster below follows an intentionalstyle visual language: minimal, dark, and quietly unsettling. A single object doing the emotional work instead of a crowded frame.

UI/UX CONCEPT, FIGMA

SynchroTrack

Mobile UI UX Flow Concept

A dynamic sonic focus companion. SynchroTrack is a concept app where sound adapts to how you want to feel: pick a target state: Calm Focus, Medium Drive or High Energy and the app plays a session tuned to it, with a running timer to track how long you stay in flow.

I designed the full first‑time flow: an onboarding splash, authentication, a mood‑selection screen built around three tactile spheres, and three distinct session screens whose gradients shift from cool and quiet to warm and driven. The color itself communicating intensity before you've read a word.

COMPONENT EXPLORATION, FIGMA

Save Button

Design System Component States

One component, different states. A single "Save" action rarely stays single — it needs a default, a lighter and darker fill for hierarchy, and a distinct filled/icon‑swapped "Saved" confirmation state.

This exercise was about component thinking rather than a one‑off screen: naming states clearly, keeping spacing and corner radius consistent across every variant, and making the saved/unsaved distinction readable at a glance — icon and label both changing together, never just one.

RoleComponent design
ToolFigma
Variants6 states, 1 component
FocusConsistency & naming
